This project is based on my personal experience of the intergenerational issues between my grandmother and her children and my observation of her personal plight, and a series of social research, to design a set of interactive products for seniors, their children and the community to connect seniors, children and the community in a rational way, with the hope that it will allow seniors to build good connections with their family community and solve intergenerational issues to some extent.
