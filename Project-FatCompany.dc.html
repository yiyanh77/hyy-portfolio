is fat really worthless?

Speculative Design on FatFuture Design
7 pages · scroll down ↓
People’s negative attitude towards fat is influenced not only by health concerns but also by societal aesthetics and body image anxieties. However, through in-depth research, I discovered the untapped potential of fat in energy storage, biomaterials, cosmetic medicine, and food manufacturing. Inspired by this, I envisioned a future where fat is no longer despised but valued as a precious resource. I aimed to shift people’s perspective from fat anxiety to cherishing and utilizing it. Through this speculative design, I hope to encourage a reexamination of fat, uncover its hidden value, and offer a new perspective on societal issues such as body image anxieties and fat aversion.
