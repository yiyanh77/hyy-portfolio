To attract young people’s attention to traditional Chinese opera, I have designed an exhibition with the theme of “Langxiang” at Tian Le Yuan Grand Theater. The exhibition revolves around the classic opera “Scattering Flowers by Celestial Maidens” and consists of three main components: interactive education, dynamic posters, and video interactive installations. Through these display formats, I aim to provide young people with a deeper understanding of the Langxiang culture.
