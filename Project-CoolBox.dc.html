The Cool Box Plan

The Cool Box Plan is a initiative focusing on thermal comfort inequality. It aims to raise awareness about frequent heatstroke deaths in recent years through web design. The highlight of this project is the development of a “Cool Box,” a modular, movable shade-providing structure designed to protect outdoor workers from the heat, addressing a significant social concern.
