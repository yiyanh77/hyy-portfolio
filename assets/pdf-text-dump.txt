extraction and filling, as it can make the body more conforming to
one's own beauty ideals, thereby increasing self-confidence and a sense
of value. However, this fulfillment may be superficial and temporary,
changing with the shifting societal values. Additionally, it may cause
some moral controversies and aversion. Some people may see it as a
violation of human dignity and bodily integrity or as a vain and
ostentatious behavior. These can affect human respect and recognition
for oneself and others, lowering self-confidence and a sense of value.
Fat company could provide a new source of income for
individuals who are striving to meet basic needs such
as food, shelter, and clothing. By selling their excess fat
to the company, they could improve their financial
situation and have access to resources to meet their
physiological needs. However, this satisfaction may be
temporary, as long-term fat extraction may pose
potential health risks and side effects.
For individuals at the self-actualization needs level, the new resources and technolo-
gies provided by the fat company may inspire their creativity and potential. This is
because the company can precisely extract and fill fat, which can help the body get
closer to its potential and achieve optimal status.
Moreover, individuals may express their potential and creativity by creating innova-
tive, unique, and delicious lab-grown meat. For example, using 3D printing technolo-
gy to create meat with a completely authentic texture and composition.
The social needs of humans can be partially fulfilled
through fat extraction and filling, as it can make the body

while, others may reject lab-grown meat to gain a sense
of belonging to a different group.

Research has shown that these intuitions were correct: today we know that some-
thing called exposure therapy can effectively reduce death anxiety. Exposure thera-
py involves deliberately facing situations, places or images that remind you of what
you fear. In general, it works by providing opportunities to learn to tolerate the
anxiety that comes about when you face these things. In relation to death anxiety
specifically, facing reminders of death also helps to normalise death and cultivate an
acceptance of mortality.
Two thousand years ago, Stoic philosophers such as Seneca
already understood the importance of facing reminders of
death in order to overcome anxiety about it. This practice has
occasionally been referred to as memento mori, a Latin phrase that
translates to ‘remember you will die’.
