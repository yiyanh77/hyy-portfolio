Kill Myself Three Times

Experimental Film on Death
6 pages · scroll down ↓
To confront the persistent death anxiety that has haunted me for years, I decided to create an experimental video. In this video, I chose to repeatedly simulate my own death as a means to reexamine my emotions and psychological experiences. The inspiration for this approach is drawn from exposure therapy, and through three self-inflicted “deaths,” I aim to comprehensively explore the emotions associated with death from various perspectives. During the design process, I conducted extensive literature research to gain a deeper understanding of how different cultures and historical eras perceive and approach death. These research findings have provided profound insights and inspiration for the creation of the video.
