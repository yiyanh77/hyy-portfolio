Mortise and Tenon

A hardcover book on Chinese mortise-and-tenon joinery. Tenon is yang, mortise is yin: the two halves complete each other, and the whole structure holds without a single nail. The book is built in three parts — the carpenter's tools, the joint families, and the buildings and bracket sets they make possible — with isometric line drawings set against photography of the tools themselves.
