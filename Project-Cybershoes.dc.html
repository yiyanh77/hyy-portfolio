walking, with your feet
Cybershoes XR

2024–2025 Nov–Jan
This study presents research on whether and how a new kind of motion device, called Cybershoes, could provide more immersive VR running games than the traditionally used handheld controllers by incorporating the users’ foot movements into the virtual environment. These results show that Cybershoes greatly improve the degree of immersion, especially in physical presence, which resemble the natural actions of walking and running very closely. Based on participants’ feedback, we identified several factors influencing immersion and potential future applications for this new locomotion method.
